# Whee Brand

## Logo

- `assets/brand/whee-logo.png` is the primary logo for the app, splash screen,
  and branded materials.
- `assets/brand/app-icon.png` is a square crop of the supplied image for app
  launchers and the web favicon.
- Use the supplied artwork without stretching, recoloring, or adding effects.

## Idea

Whee makes everyday prices easier to compare. The falling chart and coins make
price changes tangible; keep the voice clear, useful, and upbeat without
promising that every nearby price is the lowest.

## Voice

- Helpful: “Compare prices near you.”
- Plainspoken: explain units and dates wherever prices are compared.
- Trustworthy: describe crowdsourced prices as reported, not guaranteed.

Suggested short line: **Everyday prices, made clearer.**
